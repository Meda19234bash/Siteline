SITELINE By Yashmit

Free-for-all in a desert market town. Everyone is a target, friends and bots alike.

UPDATE 7 What’s new
  • Online play on school and work Wi-Fi now goes through a relay when a direct connection is blocked.
  • Text chat in the lobby and in matches (press Enter).
  • Bots glow with a red outline, and they’re a bit easier: slower reactions, shakier aim, less game sense.
  • A new pump-action shotgun with a spread-ring crosshair.
  • Reloads show the magazine swap clearly.

Building the town…

Online matches run peer to peer: one player hosts and shares a 5-letter code, everyone else joins with it. Keep the host’s tab open.

This game needs a keyboard and mouse. Open it on a laptop or desktop to play.

Field manual

  • +20 points for every elimination, +10 for an assist (25+ damage to someone another player finishes within 10 s).
  • First to the point target wins, or whoever leads when the clock runs out.
  • You respawn after 3 seconds with 2 seconds of spawn protection. Firing ends it early.
  • Health comes back after 4 seconds out of the fight. Ammo is unlimited; reloads aren’t.
  • Fallen players drop a green supply case 70% of the time. Walk over it to heal to full and refill your ammo instantly.
  • There’s no sprint: pull out your knife (slot 3) to run fastest. A knife hit from behind does double damage.
